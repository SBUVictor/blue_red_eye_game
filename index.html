How to play

Read, Answer, Ride

Each stage shows a short comedy scene. Alphabetic letters can be split into stable red and blue colors. Read the scene, answer one A/B/C/D question, then move on.

A wrong answer gives you a joke and sends you back to reread. A correct answer opens the next stage. There are no points, lives, timers, streaks, or medical claims.

Use the settings to switch to plain text, adjust text length, reduce glow, change font spacing, and tune the red/blue ratio.
